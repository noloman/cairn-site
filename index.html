Cairns

Freeze a session. Pick it up weeks later.

Save every tab in a Space as a named Cairn, and restore it when the project comes back. Each page reopens where you left it, and restoring never closes what you already have open.

Cairn names them for you from the tab titles, and you can attach typed notes or Apple Pencil ink.

Spaces

Two identities, side by side.

Every Space has its own tabs, its own search engine and its own cookie store, so you can be signed in to the same site as two different people. Put a page from one Space next to a page from another in split view.

On-device AI

Summaries that never leave the iPad.

Summarise a page, ask a question across the tabs in a Space, compare pages in a table or tidy loose tabs into Cairns. It all runs on the iPad with Apple Intelligence, so it works on a plane and no page text is sent anywhere.

On an iPad without Apple Intelligence, you can download an open model instead. Cairn tells you the size before anything is fetched.

Privacy

Nothing about your browsing is ours to see.

No account
There is nothing to sign up for, and no server of ours between you and the web.
No analytics
No tracking, no crash reporter that sees your pages, no ads. This site has none either.
Blocking built in
Ads and trackers are blocked before the request is made, and cookie pop-ups are answered “reject all”.
Your iCloud, not ours
Spaces, tabs and Cairns sync through your private iCloud database. History and page text stay on the iPad.

Before you install

What you need, and what it costs.

iPadOS 26 or later
Cairn is made for iPad, with a sidebar, a command bar, split view, Stage Manager and Apple Pencil.
Apple Intelligence, or a downloaded model
Apple Intelligence needs an iPad with M1 or later, or A17 Pro. Other iPads can download an open model (about 1 GB) the first time they use an AI feature.
21 days with everything
Every feature is unlocked from the first launch. After that, Cairn keeps working with one Space.
