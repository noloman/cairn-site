A browser for research you come back to

Cairn is a private web browser for iPad. It keeps each Space's sign-ins apart, freezes a session into a Cairn you can restore weeks later, and runs its summaries on the iPad itself.

What it does

  • Spaces with their own tabs and their own cookie store.
  • Cairns: save everything open in a Space, with scroll positions, and bring it back later.
  • On-device AI with Apple Intelligence: page summaries, questions across your tabs, Tidy and Compare.
  • Blocking of ads, trackers and cookie banners, with consent pop-ups answered "reject all".
  • A sidebar, a command bar, split view and Apple Pencil highlighting.

Cairn needs iPadOS 26 or later. The AI features need an iPad that supports Apple Intelligence.
