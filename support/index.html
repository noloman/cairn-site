Common questions

Why don't I see Summarise or Ask?
They need Apple Intelligence, which needs a supported iPad with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.

How do I restore my purchase?
It comes back by itself on any iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. If it doesn't, tap the trial banner at the bottom of the sidebar to open the unlock screen, then tap Restore.

A site doesn't work properly.
Tap the padlock in the address bar and turn blocking off for that site. Other sites keep their protection.

My Spaces don't appear on my other iPad.
Check that both iPads use the same Apple Account and that iCloud is on for Cairn in Settings. History and offline pages never sync, by design.

How do I make Cairn my default browser?
Not yet available: Apple has to approve Cairn as a default browser first.
