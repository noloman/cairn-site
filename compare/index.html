An iPad browser for people who used Arc.

Arc is in maintenance and never shipped a real iPad app. Its two closest replacements, Cairn and Orion, take different bets. This page is honest about both, and about what Safari already does well.

Where Safari already wins

Safari is free, made by Apple, and its Resume Browsing and Notify Me in iPadOS 27 cover a lot of ground. If separating identities and picking a project back up by name aren't a daily need, Safari is the simpler choice.

Where Orion already wins

Orion is free with paid tiers from $5 a month, syncs bookmarks from Chrome and Firefox, and supports some browser extensions, which Cairn does not. If extensions matter more than on-device AI, Orion is worth trying first.

Where Cairn is different

Cairn is built around two habits research actually has: keeping identities apart, and putting a project down for weeks without losing the tabs. Its AI runs only on the iPad, with no account and no subscription, ever.
